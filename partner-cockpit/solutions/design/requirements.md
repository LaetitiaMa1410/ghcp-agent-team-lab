# Reference — Requirements (use case B)

> One possible result of steps 1b–1c (interview + requirements). Items marked 🗣 came from the interview with Jonas, not from `USE-CASE.md`.

## User stories and acceptance criteria

**US-1 · Situation** — *As Jonas, I want a short, reliable picture of the partner, so that I don't re-read all files.*
- [ ] Situation in 5 statements, each labelled (fact / customer statement / assessment / hypothesis / open question).
- [ ] Official numbers are used (27,900 households); guesses are labelled or dropped 🗣.
- [ ] Contradictions are resolved with *newer beats older* and the contradiction is mentioned (launch Q2 2028) 🗣.

**US-2 · Who decides** — *As Jonas, I want to know who decides and what each person needs.*
- [ ] Decision map: Thomas Reuter, Dr. Katrin Albers, Melanie Kraus, supervisory board — role, need, concern.
- [ ] Dr. Albers **attends meeting 2** and her focus is cost risk 🗣.
- [ ] Council positions are described **neutrally**; rumours never appear as facts.

**US-3 · Technical fit** — *As Jonas, I want to know what we can really deliver.*
- [ ] Product per district type (own build / Contoso fiber / not yet built), with evidence.
- [ ] Recommended onboarding level fits the partner's IT; the 24/7 fault-handling gap is addressed.

**US-4 · Two scenarios and a recommendation** — *As Jonas, I want a bold and a safe option, so that the board has a real choice.*
- [ ] Bold and safe scenario, each with households and customers for **5 years**, using the consulting tool's curves 🗣 and official household numbers.
- [ ] Formula and assumptions visible; unfunded districts not counted as certain.
- [ ] A clear **recommendation** with reasoning 🗣 — conflicts between views stay visible 🗣.

**US-5 · Meeting 2 plan** — *As Jonas, I want a plan that wins the next commitment step.*
- [ ] Goal and **one concrete next commitment step** that Thomas can show the board on 10 December (e.g. letter of intent or joint workshop) 🗣.
- [ ] 5 questions, including the open FiberNext question 🗣.
- [ ] Materials: only `external` / `showable_in_meeting`; **no prices** (no pricing approval yet) 🗣.

**US-6 · Open points** — *As Jonas, I want to know what's still unclear.*
- [ ] Open questions with who can answer them.
