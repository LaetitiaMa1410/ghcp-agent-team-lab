# Notes — Meeting 1 with Stadtwerke Lindenau (2026-10-28)

*Written by Jonas Weber right after the meeting. Raw notes, not reviewed.*

Participants: Thomas Reuter (Stadtwerke), Melanie Kraus (Stadtwerke, joined for 20 min), Jonas Weber (Contoso).
Dr. Albers could not attend.

- Thomas: "We are building our own fiber network in three districts. Construction should be done in 2027 and we want to launch in 2027."
- They want to **keep their own brand** ("Lindenau.net" is the working title). Customers should not see Contoso.
- Thomas: "Our IT is built for energy. We have nobody who knows telecom billing." Melanie worried about **customer service**: "Who answers the phone when the internet is down at 9 pm?"
- They talked to **FiberNext** last month. FiberNext offered an "open access" model. Thomas didn't share details.
- Thomas asked whether Contoso could provide service in districts where they have no fiber yet — "people won't wait three years".
- My guess: the town has around **35,000 households**, so the potential is big. (Need to check.)
- I heard from a colleague that the mayor might want to **sell the network to an investor later**. Not confirmed — don't mention!
- Thomas needs something he can **show the supervisory board on 10 December**. Dr. Albers decides together with the board.
- Mood: open, but cautious. Thomas: "We cannot afford a failure — this is the town's money."
