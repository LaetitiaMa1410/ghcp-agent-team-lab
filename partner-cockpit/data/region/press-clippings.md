# Press clippings

## Lindenauer Anzeiger, 2026-09-19 — "Council says yes to fiber"
The town council approved EUR 9.5 million for the fiber network of Stadtwerke Lindenau. Managing Director Dr. Katrin Albers:
"We will build the network. For the internet service itself we are looking for a strong partner — we don't want to
reinvent everything." Construction in Weststadt and Am Mühlbach starts in spring 2027.

## Birkenfelder Rundschau, 2026-06-02 — "Two years of fiber in Birkenfeld"
Stadtwerke Birkenfeld has connected 4,100 households two years after launch, a take-up of 18 % of homes passed.
Managing director: "Starting simple via the partner portal was the right decision. We can grow into automation later."

## Allgäu Kurier, 2026-08-14 — "Stadtwerke Lindenberg stops fiber plans"
Stadtwerke Lindenberg has stopped its fiber plans after costs rose by 40 %. The supervisory board voted against the project.

## Lindenauer Anzeiger, 2026-10-05 — "FiberNext eyes the region"
The fiber company FiberNext is planning its own network in the region and is talking to several municipalities.
