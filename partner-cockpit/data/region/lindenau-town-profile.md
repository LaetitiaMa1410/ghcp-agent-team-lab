## Digital strategy "Lindenau 2030" (adopted 2025)

- Goal: gigabit-capable connections for **90 % of households by 2030**.
- The town wants digital infrastructure "in local hands" and to keep value creation in the region.
- Priority areas: schools, Gewerbepark Nord, the districts Weststadt and Am Mühlbach.

## Neighbouring towns

- **Birkenfeld** (12 km): municipal utility Stadtwerke Birkenfeld launched fiber in 2024 with a wholesale partner.
- **Lindenberg** (different town, 300 km away, often confused with Lindenau).
