# Town council of Lindenau — minutes (extract), meeting of 2026-09-18

## Item 7: Gigabit expansion "Gigabit Lindenau"

The council approved co-funding of EUR 9.5 million for the fiber build by Stadtwerke Lindenau in the districts
**Weststadt** and **Am Mühlbach** (vote: 27 yes, 9 no, 2 abstentions).

The funding application for the district **Altstadt** is still pending at the state funding agency; a decision is expected in Q2 2027.

Mayor: "Fast internet is part of basic services. The network must remain in municipal hands."

The opposition group criticised the cost risk: "The utility should not become a telecom company with taxpayers' money."
The council asked Stadtwerke Lindenau to present an **operating and marketing model** to the supervisory board by Q1 2027.
