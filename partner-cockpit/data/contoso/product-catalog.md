# Contoso Wholesale — fiber wholesale products for partners

> ⚠️ **INTERNAL.** Prices and conditions in this file are internal and must not appear in documents for the customer without approval by pricing.

| Product | What it is | Monthly price per line | One-time fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Fiber Access Basic** | Layer-2 bitstream access on Contoso fiber, up to 1 Gbit/s | EUR 24.50 | EUR 49 |
| **Fiber Access Plus** | Fiber Access Basic + managed router + 24/7 fault handling by Contoso | EUR 31.00 | EUR 89 |
| **VDSL Interim Access** | Layer-2 access on Contoso copper (VDSL) where no fiber is available yet; can be migrated to fiber at no cost | EUR 17.90 | EUR 29 |
| **Network Operations Service** | Contoso operates the partner's *own* fiber network (monitoring, fault handling, field service) | from EUR 2.10 per home passed and month | project-based |

## Commitment discounts (on monthly line prices)

| Committed lines after 3 years | Discount |
| --- | --- |
| 1,000+ | 5 % |
| 3,000+ | 10 % |
| 6,000+ | 15 % |

Missing the commitment leads to a shortfall payment of 50 % of the discount difference.

## Notes

- Partners always sell under **their own brand**; Contoso stays invisible to end customers.
- Fiber Access products are only available where Contoso has fiber (`contoso_ftth_homes_passed`).
- For networks built by the partner itself, Contoso offers the Network Operations Service.
