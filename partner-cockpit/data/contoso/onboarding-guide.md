# Partner onboarding guide

Partners can start at three integration levels. They can move up a level later.

| Level | How orders work | Good for | Prerequisites | Typical time to first order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Portal** | Orders and fault tickets manually in the Contoso partner portal | up to ~60 orders per month | Telecom registration with the regulator, a customer service team, a simple billing solution (can be a SaaS tool) | 3 months |
| **2 · Bulk & API light** | CSV bulk orders + status API | up to ~400 orders per month | Level 1 + an IT person who can run simple interfaces | 6 months |
| **3 · Fully integrated** | Full API integration into the partner's OSS/BSS | any volume | Telecom billing and order management system (OSS/BSS), dedicated integration team | 9–12 months |

## Mandatory for every partner

- **Fault handling 24/7**: either own hotline or Contoso "Fiber Access Plus" (fault handling included).
- Signed wholesale framework agreement and data processing agreement.

## Lessons learned

- Partners without telecom IT that start at level 3 have a high risk of delays.
- Most municipal utilities start at level 1 and move to level 2 after 12–18 months.
