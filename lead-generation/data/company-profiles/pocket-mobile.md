# Pocket Mobile Ltd

- Website: https://pocketmobile.example
- Headquarters: United Kingdom

## About
Mobile virtual network operator (MVNO) focusing on prepaid SIMs for travelers and young customers.

## Products and services
- Prepaid mobile plans
- eSIM travel data packs

## Customers
- Travelers
- Students

## Network and partnerships
Has no own radio network. Relies on wholesale mobile access and international roaming agreements; currently tendering for a new European roaming partner.
