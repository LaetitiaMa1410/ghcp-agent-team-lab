# Nordwave Networks GmbH

- Website: https://nordwave.example
- Headquarters: Germany

## About
Regional fiber-to-the-home internet service provider serving 350,000 households in Northern Germany. Fast-growing, currently expanding into Denmark.

## Products and services
- FTTH broadband for residential customers
- Business internet access for SMEs
- Local data center colocation (2 sites)

## Customers
- Residential households
- Municipal utilities
- Regional SMEs

## Network and partnerships
Operates its own access network but buys upstream IP transit from two international carriers. Publicly announced plans to double backbone capacity to Hamburg and Frankfurt in 2027.
